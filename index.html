Why no background colour, opacity or corner radius?

Owlbear Rodeo's native token labels only expose text style properties. Padding is a single value applied to every side, and line height controls the vertical space around each line. Lower values of both shrink the label background. Background colour, opacity and corner radius are drawn by Owlbear Rodeo and cannot be changed by extensions without replacing the label.
